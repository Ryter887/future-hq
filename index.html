⚖️ Law Lab

Practise fair reasoning. This is a fictional learning exercise, not legal advice.

Challenge 001: The broken classroom window

A window is found broken after the break. Alex says they saw Bruno near the window. Bruno says they were picking up a pen. Carlos says he saw Alex throw a ball, but does not know whether it hit the window.

Your review tasks

  • Fact: Carlos says he saw Alex throw a ball.
  • Unknown: Whether the ball hit the window.
  • Assumption to avoid: That Alex threw the ball at Bruno.
  • Better argument: The evidence needs checking before responsibility can be established.
